/goal Use $advisor-challenger to measure how much $shard-advise adds to the already-optimized MODEL_DIR decoder at decode batch DECODE_BATCH: ship the best measured decoder, or record a contribution of zero with numbers. Report the result as a FULL-MODEL estimate before and after, with its uncertainty band.

Read .agents/skills/advisor-challenger/SKILL.md and follow it — it holds the method, the chain definition and the traps. Artifacts go under doc/advisor_challenger/.

Use $shard-advise for SETUP ONLY: build/pin ttnn-advise per its SETUP.md and export TTMLIR_ADVISOR_HOME. Do NOT use its advise_decoder.py as your capture target — it is model-specific and constructs the decoder with CLASS DEFAULTS, which traced the wrong precision on a previous model and cost -10%. Copy scripts/capture_template.py instead.

This is a CONTRIBUTION MEASUREMENT: the frozen incumbent is the control, so the delta IS the result and a no-change outcome is a publishable zero. Suppressing a candidate understates the contribution — measure the doubtful ones.

NON-NEGOTIABLE — getting any of these wrong makes the result wrong rather than merely suboptimal, and the gate checks each:

1. Everything at decode batch DECODE_BATCH, recorded as decode_batch, requested_decode_batch and capture_batch.
2. Freeze the incumbent BEFORE the capture: captured_at must be after measured_at, or the control is contaminated.
3. Time with scripts/harness_template.py — copy it, fill its two model hooks, do NOT write your own harness. It fixes the protocol (>=10 untimed warm-ups, n=5 blocks of >=50 replays, incumbent_ms = MEDIAN not min) and emits the signposts that bound the profile to one replay. Measure every candidate with it too, in a FRESH process: a candidate timed after the incumbent is merely warmer.
4. shipped_policy and shipped_weight_dtypes from what EXECUTED, never constructor_defaults. Capture through capture_template.py so traced_dtypes.json records what you traced and which advisor built the advice.
5. Run scripts/reconcile.py per layer kind WITH --incumbent incumbent.json, and use its ranking. Never hand-author its output; if it aborts, fix its input. If it reports DEGRADED, stop and fix the inputs first.
6. Obey feasibility.verdict: not_measurable means do NOT screen — report zero with the arithmetic; aggregate_only means apply the top chains TOGETHER first, because screened alone each returns zero whatever the advice says. A win below the harness noise floor is not attributable to the advice.
7. Ship iff every candidate repeat beats every incumbent repeat, then confirm in a fresh process.
8. Decide across layer kinds on the PER-MODEL number (advisor_removes_per_model_us), not the per-layer one: layer counts differ by kind. Quote the model estimate with model_estimate.uncertainty_per_model_us.
9. A shipped change needs a REAL-weight oracle; record oracle_weights. A synthetic-weight PCC does not bound the real-weight PCC when the policy quantises to BFLOAT4_B, and an oracle comparing the implementation against itself cannot fail.
10. Keep every losing knob default-off so rejected candidates stay re-measurable.
11. Never sweep only at or below an advised core count — it is one candidate, not a recommendation.

Report, do not screen: model_estimate.layer_handoff (a DRAM round trip between layers belongs to whoever built the decoder) and advised_boundaries.us_advisor_agrees (real conversion time the advisor endorses, so removing it is your idea, not its contribution).

Then run .agents/prompts/model_bringup_multigoal/02b-advisor-challenger.check.sh MODEL_DIR and resolve what it reports.
