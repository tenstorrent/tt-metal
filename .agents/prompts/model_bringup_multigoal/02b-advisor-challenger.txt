/goal Use $advisor-challenger to make the finished MODEL_DIR optimized decoder faster than it already is, using $shard-advise as a challenger that must win on measurement — or prove with numbers that it cannot be beaten.

Read .agents/skills/advisor-challenger/SKILL.md FIRST and follow it — it has the detail and the traps. Use
$shard-advise for SETUP and capture mechanics only; this stage replaces its "how to incorporate" part.
Ship a NEW decoder measured to be the best of the incumbent and everything the advisor suggested. A
NO-CHANGE outcome is a valid result — record it with numbers; never manufacture a change.

All artifacts go under doc/advisor_challenger/. The gate 02b-advisor-challenger.check.sh enforces each of:

0. BATCH + ORDER. Everything runs at decode batch DECODE_BATCH. incumbent.json records decode_batch,
   requested_decode_batch (both = DECODE_BATCH) and measured_at; each report.json records capture_batch and
   captured_at, which must be AFTER measured_at.

1. FREEZE THE INCUMBENT, before the advisor runs. Its own perf harness at DECODE_BATCH, >=3 repeats, into
   incumbent.json: incumbent_ms = BEST repeat, noise_floor_ms = the spread. Record shipped_policy,
   shipped_weight_dtypes and shipped_policy_source naming the artifact they came from (final tt-perf-report CSV or
   selected candidate JSON) — NEVER resolved_policy.constructor_defaults (class defaults). Save the
   incumbent's op-level tt-perf-report CSV; every ranking below is computed from it.

2. CAPTURE ONCE PER LAYER KIND, AT THE SHIPPED PRECISION, building the target with the shipped policy
   (template scripts/capture_template.py; advise_qwen.py is the only correct example). Pass
   --pipeline-options allow-bf16-dram-sharded-matmul=true if any traced weight is bf16. Record traced_weight_dtypes and
   dram_sharded_considered; if 0, a dram_sharded_zero_cause of wrong_precision_capture or
   bf16_eligibility_default. Note uncapturable kinds (sparse_matmul/SSM are terminal) and their layer share.

3. RECONCILE IN CHAINS. Run scripts/reconcile.py against the incumbent CSV: it groups advised ops into
   chains and thresholds on the chain's SUMMED window share (OPT-003).

4. SCREEN each material chain AS ONE UNIT, one variable per measurement, against the frozen incumbent.
   Every kept candidate records its own tt-perf-report CSV as perf_report, plus the incumbent's oracle at
   its own PCC bar. Every chain ends with a measured number or below_threshold + its summed share.

5. COMBINE — screening alone does not find the best decoder, because chains interact. Measure the
   cumulative set of screened winners plus pairwise combinations of the top chains. Record EVERY measured
   set in final.json.combination.measured_sets with its ms and oracle_passed; also record best_single_ms.
   best_set is the best MEASURED set, never an inferred one.

6. INVARIANT: final_ms <= incumbent_ms AND <= best_single_ms, within noise_floor_ms. Ties to the INCUMBENT.

7. ITERATE, BOUNDED. After applying winners, RE-PROFILE and re-run reconcile.py on the new CSV to re-rank
   what remains; every iteration after the first records reranked_from, and all record a trigger.
   Re-capture only after a topology rewrite changing an op's shape; max 3 captures per layer kind.

USE THE ADVICE AS RECALL, NOT GEOMETRY. compute_config/math_fidelity in final_ir.mlir is TRACED STATE,
NOT ADVICE; overriding it is mandatory.

DELIVERABLES under doc/advisor_challenger/: incumbent.json, shard_advise/<layer_kind>/{report.json,
final_ir.mlir}, reconciliation.json, final.json, README.md, tracy/ (tt-perf-report outputs only, no raw
Tracy captures) — plus the updated tt/optimized_decoder.py.

Finish by running .agents/prompts/model_bringup_multigoal/02b-advisor-challenger.check.sh MODEL_DIR
