Gemma3 decode perf summary  (GATE_OPT=performance, batch-1, ISL 128 / OSL 200)
Hardware: Blackhole. "1 chip" = one chip of the P300; "2 chip" = one P300 card (2 chips) TP=2.
Scope: gemma3-12b (1 chip & 2 chip) and gemma3-27b (2 chip) only.

============================================================================
variant      hardware        TTFT(ms)  decode ms/tok   tok/s/user   notes
gemma3-12b   1x P300 (1chip)   83.49      34.71          28.81       ODS already default (single-chip)
gemma3-12b   1x P300 (2chip)   67.74      34.01          29.40       baseline (host sampling)
gemma3-12b   1x P300 (2chip)     -          -            37.56       + TT_GEMMA3_ODS=1  (+27.5%)
gemma3-27b   1x P300 (2chip)  124.12      62.66          15.47*      baseline (host sampling; *15.96 prior run)
gemma3-27b   1x P300 (2chip)     -          -            17.76       + TT_GEMMA3_ODS=1  (+14.8%)

ODS = on-device force-argmax for greedy decode (env: TT_GEMMA3_ODS=1). Accuracy gate PASS on
12b 2-chip: 200/200 generated tokens identical host-argmax vs on-device argmax.

Key reads:
- Biggest 2-chip win: removing per-token host logit round-trip via ODS (12b +27.5%, 27b +14.8%).
  Without ODS, 12b 2-chip (29.4) barely beats 1-chip (28.81) because CCL + host RT cancel TP.
  With ODS, 12b 2-chip (37.56) clearly beats 1-chip.
- CCL knob TT_CCL_CHUNKS=1 gave +3.7% on 12b without ODS; absorbed / negligible once ODS is on.
- Prefetcher parked: gemma3 dims (3840/15360) need ring-aligned padding (+~14% weight DRAM),
  poor ROI vs ODS.
- Matmul still ~60-75% of device time after ODS; remaining headroom is matmul BW / imbalance.

Notes:
- "dev-compute ceiling" = from the per-op tracy profile (layers only). Real traced number is
  below it because of non-layer ops (embedding, final norm, lm_head, on-device argmax sampling)
  plus residual reshards and the small dispatch that survives tracing.
- matmul is 59-75% of on-device decode time -> matmul kernel efficiency is the single biggest
  lever; everything else (norms, sdpa, rope, cache) is small individually.
- On 2-chip, dev0 (host-attached / MMIO chip) runs the same matmuls ~20% slower than dev1
  (521 vs 434 us/layer on 27b), and TP is gated by the slower chip -> chip imbalance + CCL are
  the 2-chip-specific costs, not the matmul core grids (grid narrowing was tested and rejected).
