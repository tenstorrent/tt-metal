Gemma3 decode perf summary  (GATE_OPT=performance, batch-1, ISL 128 / OSL 200)
Hardware: Blackhole. "1 chip" = one chip of the P300; "2 chip" = one P300 card (2 chips) TP=2.
Scope: gemma3-12b (1 chip & 2 chip) and gemma3-27b (2 chip) only.

============================================================================
END-TO-END TRACED PERF (production path: trace ON, 200 tok, batch-1)
============================================================================
variant      hardware        TTFT(ms)  decode ms/tok   tok/s/user   dev-compute ceiling t/s/u
gemma3-12b   1x P300 (1chip)   83.49      34.71          28.81            33.4
gemma3-12b   1x P300 (2chip)   67.74      34.01          29.40            40.8
gemma3-27b   1x P300 (2chip)  124.12      62.66          15.96            21.8

Key reads:
- 12b 2-chip (29.4) is only ~2% faster than 12b 1-chip (28.81): the CCL tax (~79 us/layer:
  AllGather 52 + ReduceScatter 27) nearly cancels the benefit of splitting the matmuls across
  2 chips. 2-chip only clearly wins on TTFT (67.7 vs 83.5 ms). For pure decode throughput,
  1-chip 12b is the efficient point.
- 12b 1-chip runs at 28.81 vs a 33.4 ceiling -> ~86% of the layer-compute ceiling: decode is
  matmul/device-bound, NOT dispatch-bound, on the traced single-chip path.
- 12b 2-chip runs at 29.4 vs a 40.8 ceiling -> ~72%: 2-chip is more CCL/dispatch-bound (idle
  gaps waiting on the all-reduce + host-attached-chip imbalance).
- 27b 2-chip runs at 15.96 vs a 21.8 ceiling -> ~73%: matmul 71% of device time + CCL 13% tax.

Notes:
- "dev-compute ceiling" = from the per-op tracy profile (layers only). Real traced number is
  below it because of non-layer ops (embedding, final norm, lm_head, on-device argmax sampling)
  plus residual reshards and the small dispatch that survives tracing.
- matmul is 59-75% of on-device decode time -> matmul kernel efficiency is the single biggest
  lever; everything else (norms, sdpa, rope, cache) is small individually.
- On 2-chip, dev0 (host-attached / MMIO chip) runs the same matmuls ~20% slower than dev1
  (521 vs 434 us/layer on 27b), and TP is gated by the slower chip -> chip imbalance + CCL are
  the 2-chip-specific costs, not the matmul core grids (grid narrowing was tested and rejected).
