The sampled `binary-bcast` semantic and `unarymaxmin` handwritten failures
show `lreg-pressure-exceeded`. The `binary-bcast` ON allocator also refuses a
hard-LREG form at graph collection; no allocator guard was relaxed. Classify
each other compile failure from its per-arm log before attributing a cause.

Among passing full 2x2 rows, compiler ON versus OFF has no large measured
regression (largest positive delta: `cbrt` +1.68%). Generated ON versus
handwritten ON is a separate comparison: `sigmoidappx-tree` +369%,
`sigmoidlut-fresh` +281%, and `gelu-fitted` +104% are the largest gaps in the
completed baseline portion. These are candidates for source/stream analysis,
not accepted slowdowns or a claim that the ON compiler pass caused them.
ReLU's faster handwritten comparator fails the current NaN contract, so its
+64% generated/hand gap is a semantic-uplift comparison, not a licensed
hand-parity target. No formal, exhaustive, or ULP campaign was run here.
