# SFPU Phase 1 — in short

Part of [#49739](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal/issues/49739) · branch `ldjurovic/sfpu_edge_cases_1` · commits `b0f4ae8`, `1291c7a`

Phase 1 applied Phase 0's reroute to the other three families: the binary, ternary and scalar suites never imported `sfpu_domains.py`, so every op fell back to a positive-only `uniform(0.1, 1.1)` and the per-op domains already sitting in `_OP_DOMAIN_REGISTRY` stayed dormant (audit finding #2). Seven float elementwise binary ops now take their registered domain — add/sub/mul/rsub/div go from **0% to 50% negative operands** and div's divisor finally spans both sides of the pole it is registered to avoid — while ternary and scalar gained the per-operand spec parameters that later phases need but have no registry entries to read yet. Two adjacent holes were closed at the same time: the scalar binops sweep an axis of `{0, 1, 2, −2, 8, 0.25}` instead of one hard-coded `2.0`, and `SfpuElwLt/Gt/Le/Ge` got `test_sfpu_binary_float_comparison`, their **first LLK-level correctness test** — driving `a<b`/`a==b`/`a>b` in equal thirds, because the exact tie is the only input where lt/gt and le/ge disagree and is exactly what a random sweep cannot deliver. Recording op arity in the registry (`sfpu_unary_ops()`) turned the unary sweep's exhaustiveness into a collection-time error, which Phase 0 had no way to check.

As in Phase 0 the mechanism was small and the fallout was most of the work — three defects that benign stimuli had been hiding. **Per-operand pairing only ever filled the first tile pair:** `face_specs` is applied positionally and is not cycled, so the fixed 8-entry list the old `_paired_two_tile_spec` returned covered tiles 0–1 and left every later pair with operand 0's distribution on both sides — measured on the eq/ne stimuli, pair 0 came out 50% equal as intended and pairs 1–15 came out **100% equal**, so `mask`, `isclose` and `eq`/`ne` were each testing one sixteenth of what they appeared to. Fixing it exposed the second: **`calculate_mask` only works for one tile pair per dest block** — the kernel hard-codes data at `dst_reg[0]` and mask at `dst_reg[32]`, ignores the forwarded dst indices and loops `tile += 2`, so tiles 2/4/6 were packed out as unmasked datacopy against a golden that masks every pair; 12 of 16 pairs failed and the test moved to a `[64, 32]` buffer. Third, **`pow` and `xlogy` needed accuracy-bounded domains** — neither had ever executed, and `pow`'s error tracks the product `b·ln a` handed to the shared exp approximation rather than either operand (`8.05` → 6.1% off against a 5% rtol), so both are capped with the measured numbers in the registry comments. Separately, neither `sfpu_binary` nor `default_spec_for_format` had ever set a seed, so the binary suite redrew its stimuli every run and a flaky variant was indistinguishable from a real finding; both drivers now seed.

**Net:** 7 binary ops gained negative-operand and pole-spanning coverage, 4 comparison ops gained their first LLK test, per-operand pairing went from 1-in-16 tile pairs to all of them, a mask-kernel limitation and two unmeasured accuracy limits were documented rather than tolerated, the binary suite became deterministic, and ternary/scalar are plumbed for Phases 2–4. Blackhole, all four suites: **4990 passed, 1700 skipped, 7 xfailed**.
