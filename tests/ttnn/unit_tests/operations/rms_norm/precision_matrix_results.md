## Why `row_scale_bias` and not just PCC

`row_scale_bias` is the mean relative error of the per-row `1/rms` factor, recovered by a
least-squares fit of `out ~ k * (x * gamma)` per row. It is the gate that matters for an RMS
norm, and **PCC is nearly blind to it**: the 16-bit-DEST sum-of-squares overestimate that
Refinement 1 diagnosed and fixed presented as PCC **0.99995** while every row was scaled
**4.8% low**. A uniform scale error is exactly what folding tile padding into the denominator,
or losing an accumulator to rounding, looks like — so it gets its own assertion.

The regressor must include gamma. Fitting `out ~ k * x` cancels to ~0 for a random-sign
gamma and reports `k ~ 0` (bias ~ -1.0) for a perfectly correct kernel.
