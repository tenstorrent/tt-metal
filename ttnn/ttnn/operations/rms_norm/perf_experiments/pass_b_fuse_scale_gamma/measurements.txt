5. THE FUSED SPELLING IS NUMERICALLY WRONG AT blk == 8
=============================================================================
BinaryFpu<x,stat,Mul,Col> + DestReuseBinary<gamma_full,Mul,DEST_TO_SRCB> + PackTile at
block_size 8 (== DEST_AUTO_LIMIT for bf16 half-sync) gives pcc 0.987 / rel-RMS 0.11 at
every geometry tried (1x16, 1x32, 8x16, 8x8).  The same block_size is CORRECT for the
two-chain baseline, so it is specific to DestReuseBinary occupying all 8 DEST lanes.
Not root-caused: the variant is a measured regression at every block_size where it IS
correct, so the cliff only reinforces the verdict.  If anyone revisits DEST-reuse here,
cap block_size at DEST_AUTO_LIMIT/2 and re-gate.

6. WHAT THE 1.59x IS MADE OF (focus point)
=============================================================================
14050  baseline                    per-tile reserve/push, per-tile tile_regs handshake,
                                   one hoisted init+reconfig per CALL
10710  + one reserve/push per CALL (Upfront/AtEnd, blk still 1)      -> 3.3 us
 8860  + 4 DEST lanes per outer iter (blk=4, PerChunk lifecycles)    -> 1.9 us more
13676  reconfig ablation alone (drop the 3 provably-inert reconfigs)  ->  0.4 us
=> the win is the per-TILE fixed cost (CB flow-control ops + the MATH<->PACK DEST
   handshake + per-element init), amortised over blk tiles.  It is the same mechanism as
   examples/compute_block_size, one level down (DEST lanes, not chain calls).
   The reconfig ablation is real but small here (2.7%) -- pass B only has 6 reconfigs
   per block and 3 of them are genuinely needed.
