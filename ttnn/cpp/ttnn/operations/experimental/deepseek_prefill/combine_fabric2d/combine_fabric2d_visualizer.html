Why `expert_offsets` matters

An expert region is ordered by origin chip. Both ends calculate a chunk's page range locally, so the forwarding buffer can remain dense without exchanging dynamic chunk sizes.

run_begin(d, e) = expert_offsets[d][e] run_end(d, e) = expert_offsets[d+1][e] or region_offset[e] + token_count[e] output_page = token_idx * num_experts_per_tok + topk_idx

The two independent rings

L1 ring (8 slots) reader fills slot → filled semaphore → sender transmits sender flushes L1 read → freed semaphore → reader reuses slot Forwarding DRAM region upstream Fabric write → fwd_arrived semaphore → downstream reader relays

For TILE input, untilizer cores sit before the reader and turn tile rows into row-major token rows. The routing logic after that point is the same.
