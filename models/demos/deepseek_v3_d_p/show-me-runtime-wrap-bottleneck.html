Where to optimize

Recover groups first; fuse plumbing second

Priority 1: retain multiple recurrence groups across the wrap at 5K. This attacks the evidence-backed 1.47 ms loss.

Priority 2: narrow final-state transport, fuse the affine micrograph, and compact convolution-carry exchange. Together these target the roughly fixed 0.30 ms.
