# Known issues, full text: xing40_a4b_d_p

`known_issues.md` holds the short form of each entry and points here for the full text.

## The auto checks' mistake sweep lets 1 % noise through on mHC coefficients

- **The auto checks' mistake sweep lets 1 % noise through on mHC coefficients.** Symptom: on the Xing4.0 layer-0 attn_hc golden ([S, 24] = pre | post | comb), `BRINGUP_IMPL=mutations` reports noise1e-2 SLIPPED (whole-output rel 0.007 under the calibrated limit 0.0148). Cause: the bf16-everywhere precision model rounds the comb logits (up to ~30) to bf16, so its comb rel is 0.0077 and the rel limit sits at the cap; the noise hides in comb, while post (mean 0.013) moves by rel 0.09 and goes negative. Fix: keep checks="auto" and add per-part rel L2 (pre 0.006, post 0.03, comb 0.012 vs the CPU step), comb column sums within 0.01 of 1 (the Sinkhorn's last step), and the value ranges; the reviewed test runs its own sweep under mutations (`xing40_a4b_d_p/tests/bringup/test_c_dense_attn_hc.py`). Found: xing40_a4b_d_p C.dense.attn_hc test.

## A correct attention device is noisier than the sweep's 1 % noise mutation

- **A correct attention device is noisier than the sweep's 1 % noise mutation.** Symptom: on the Xing4.0 layer-2 attention golden ([2048, 3584], s4096 chunk 1), `BRINGUP_IMPL=mutations` reports noise1e-2 SLIPPED (rel 0.0100 under the calibrated limit 0.0135), and the device module (ring_mla fork, HiFi4, fp32 dest) already measures rel 0.0108 vs the CPU step, so no size limit can separate them. Cause: layer 2's sharper scores give the bf16-everywhere model rel 0.0068 (layer 0: 0.0028), and the device is 1.6x that. Fix: check where the error lies. Take the error vs the CPU step outside the top 1024 right singular vectors of the CPU output (0.9993 of its energy): device 0.0027, bf16 model 0.0021, noise 0.0085. Limit 0.006 on the golden, chunk0 and layer39 inputs; the test runs its own sweep under mutations (`xing40_a4b_d_p/tests/bringup/test_c_moe_attention.py`). Cache anything derived from the Expect object on that object, not in a module global: the precompile collect pass builds its own Expect in the same process, and a global cache kept its basis (bf16 control 0.0063 instead of 0.0020). Applies to: models/demos/xing40_a4b_d_p/tests/bringup/test_c_moe_attention.py, ttnn/ttnn/bringup/sdpa. Seen at: 744ed21d97f. Check: on the 4x2 LoudBox (8 x p150b), run test_c_moe_attention.py on the device (fork ring_mla, HiFi4 + fp32 DEST) and compare the whole rel vs the CPU step (was 0.0108) and the off-subspace rel (was 0.0027, limit 0.006). Unverified since: 864ec8b0891. Found: xing40_a4b_d_p C.moe.attention test.

## ring_mla at HiFi2 shrinks attention output norms past the component limits, yet end-to-end accuracy holds

- **ring_mla at HiFi2 shrinks attention output norms past the component limits, yet end-to-end accuracy holds.** Symptom: Xing4.0 dense MLA through `ttnn.bringup.ring_mla` (fp32 DEST, streaming, K 576 / V 512) at HiFi2 fails the frozen MoE-layer attention test (layer 2): median row norm ratio -0.0068 (limit 0.004), min row ratio 0.984 (limit 0.985), rel 0.0137 > 0.0135. HiFi4 there gives -0.0026 / 0.990 / 0.0108. Cause: HiFi2 drops operand mantissa bits and biases the products toward zero, as for the dense MLP, and layer 2's sharp scores magnify it. Fix: none needed end to end: rung last and s56320 at HiFi2 (q64 / k256) stay within 1e-3 of HiFi4 on every layer, final hidden and logits, top5 1.0 (min layer 0.9919 vs 0.9923 at 56k); the owner accepted HiFi2 on those numbers and dropped the layer-2 attention component test from that perf gate. Timing per layer (chunk 5120 after a 51200 prefix): HiFi4 q32 / k256 25.9 ms, HiFi3 20.6 ms (same accuracy as HiFi4), HiFi2 q64 / k256 17.9 ms. Math fidelity does not change the CB sizes, so the L1 limits on q / k chunk sizes are the same at every fidelity. Applies to: ttnn/ttnn/bringup/sdpa, models/demos/xing40_a4b_d_p/tt/attention.py. Seen at: 80f986420b1. Check: on the 4x2 LoudBox (8 x p150b), run the layer-2 attention component test with XING_MLA_SDPA_FIDELITY=HiFi2 and HiFi4 (were median ratio -0.0068 / -0.0026, min 0.984 / 0.990, rel 0.0137 / 0.0108) and re-time chunk 5120 after a 51200 prefix (were 17.9 / 25.9 ms). Unverified since: 864ec8b0891. Found: xing40_a4b_d_p P.1.

## HiFi2 in the fused Silu expert kernel (high_precision) biases every row low

- **HiFi2 in the fused Silu expert kernel (high_precision) biases every row low.** Symptom: Xing4.0 layer-2 routed experts through `ttnn.bringup.unified_routed_expert_moe` (bfp8 weights, bf16 x / intermediates, fp32 dest and partials) at HiFi2 keep PCC 0.99995, but the median row norm ratio is off by -0.0136 (limit 0.004), row ratios fall in [0.977, 0.995], and rel is 0.0168 against a limit of 0.0137. HiFi4 gives bias ~0 and ratios [0.995, 1.008]. Cause: HiFi2 truncates operand mantissas (see the shared "HiFi2 and bf16 intermediates bias output norms"), and the three chained matmuls (gate/up, then down) compound it. Fix: none within the frozen limits, so component tests run the experts at HiFi4 (`MAX_PRECISION`); the owner later shipped HiFi2, judged end to end (e0cf2d48638; `XING_EXPERTS_FIDELITY` default hifi2, hifi4 restores HiFi4). Applies to: unified_routed_expert_moe, models/demos/xing40_a4b_d_p/tt/settings.py, models/demos/xing40_a4b_d_p/tt/experts.py. Seen at: 864ec8b0891. Found: xing40_a4b_d_p P.3.
