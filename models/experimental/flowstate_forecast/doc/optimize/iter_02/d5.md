## 1. The fused SwiGLU at accuracy is a precision change (v1.14), not accumulation order

Correction to X3 and the R2-1x row: the single_long excursion comes from the fused SwiGLU.

**Mechanism** (`minimal_matmul(fuse_swiglu=True)`, `device/kernels/compute.cpp` `swiglu_block`; the program
factory picks compute.cpp):
- The K partial sums go to an intermediate CB, in **Float32** when fp32_dest_acc_en is set, as it is at accuracy
  (HiFi2, `_cfg`), with packer L1 accumulation.
- The bias is added with `add_tiles_bcast<ROW>` on the FPU: the fp32 intermediate and the bias are unpacked to
  srcA/srcB, which hold 19 bits (10-bit mantissa); the factory does not set UnpackToDestFp32.
- Then `silu_tile` on the gate in DST (fp32, math_approx_mode False: the accurate sigmoid), `mul_binary_tile`
  (SFPU, dst x dst, fp32), and one pack to bf16.
- Unfused (R1-1): w1 is a `ttnn.linear` with silu fused (bf16 out), w2 a `ttnn.linear` (bf16 out), and then a bf16
  `ttnn.multiply`: three bf16 roundings, but no 19-bit truncation of the pre-activation.
- So the silu is already the exact one; the candidate rounding point is the 19-bit srcA/srcB add of the bias. That
  is a reading of the kernel; it is **not measured**.
