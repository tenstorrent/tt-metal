## MoE, campaign and roadmap

- **Token-gather denoise MoE (on-device GShard capacity dispatch)** — refuted 2026-07-29; the trajectory does not converge (entropy plateaus ~0.46 against a 0.005 halt threshold, ~2/3 of requests end degenerate) and at the shipped `capacity=256` its gather/combine added ~89% MACs on an expert MAC count already equal to computing every expert densely. [optimize_perf/README.md](optimize_perf/README.md), [winter_borrow_20260727.md](optimize_perf/winter_borrow_20260727.md)
- **Wiring a fixed-step trace into the eager measured denoise path (2026-07-04 "lever 1")** — refuted; the saving is ~0.66% at 30L and a static trace forces a fixed 48-step budget that removes early halt. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **BFP8/BFP4 expert precision on the dense-128 path** — refuted 2026-07-04; expert weight traffic was only ~1.6 ms/layer and the MoE was transpose-bound, so precision cannot help there. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **bfp8 MoE experts measured properly (`DG_EXPERTS_BFP8`)** — refuted 2026-07-08; committed clean-argmax agreement 0.227 against a ≥0.95 bar (entropy PCC 0.631, accept IoU 0.501) for only +6–9% traced. [datatype_sweep/README.md](datatype_sweep/README.md)
- **Per-token `nnz=8` sparse routing on the 256 canvas** — refuted 2026-07-04; `TT_FATAL: sparsity.logical_volume() == batch_length`, because `sparse_matmul` sparsity granularity is per M-tile-group (32 tokens), not per token. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Larger prefill chunk (64/128/256) on the dense path** — refuted 2026-07-04; blocked by `num_blocks_total <= num_cores_available` core overflow. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Larger 64/128-token sparse-matmul chunks in the exact-geometry prefill MoE** — refuted 2026-07-10; PCC fell to roughly 0.64/0.48, so the 32-token chunk is retained. [optimize_perf/README.md](optimize_perf/README.md)
- **Moving MoE intermediates DRAM→L1 on the dense path** — refuted 2026-07-04; 137.09 → 135.14 ms is a 1.01× wash at PCC 1.0. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **`DG_MOE_L1` (pinning the 23 MB gather and 23 MB down MoE activation outputs in L1)** — refuted 2026-07-09; isolated micro −3.2% and bit-identical, but traced e2e @48 −0.6% / @12 +0.4%, because those DRAM round-trips are overlap-hidden under trace. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **The remaining L1-residency candidates (residual stream, attention, masks)** — closed by reasoning 2026-07-09; residual-stream L1 is net-zero without the whole stack, attention L1 is a guarded passthrough no-op with real L1-SDPA blocked by the flash CB clash, masks are ~2 MB, and every `InterleavedToSharded`+`ShardedToInterleaved` in denoise totals <3% of the step — the chunked-norm slice/concat was the lever, not conversion round-trips. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Rewriting the expert-major `Permute` in place** — refuted 2026-07-04; 0% gain, the cost just relocates, so the only way to shrink it was to compute fewer experts. [whole_gen_opprofile/README.md](optimize_perf/whole_gen_opprofile/README.md)
- **2 command queues / host-gap removal for the denoise step** — refuted 2026-07-04; the step is compute bound with negligible I/O to overlap, and the whole-run tracy `GAP_FRACTION=81%` that motivated it is a device-profiler artifact. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **OPT-004 `in0_block_w` tuning** — refuted 2026-07-04; every legal divisor of Kt=88 spanned only 0.570–0.591 ms (~3.6%) and *smaller* `in0_block_w` was marginally faster, contradicting the "larger K-block lifts DRAM burst" thesis, because those batched matmuls (M=1 tile, N=6 tiles) are per-core-launch bound. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Replacing the eight `ttnn.slice` canvas-norm calls with one `ttnn.split`** — refuted 2026-07-16; the isolated norm improved 1.430 → 1.321 ms (−7.6%) byte-identically, but full traced fixed-12 regressed 5.4344 → 5.5790 s/block (−2.6%) because trace overlap already hides the slice programs. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Bit-exact canvas-norm fusion via TTNN descriptor fusion** — refuted 2026-07-16; the stock batch reshape needs shard height 256, an eight-phase program exceeds Blackhole's kernel-config buffer (121152 B requested vs 70656 B), reusing a fused descriptor deadlocks in an inter-phase semaphore wait, a fresh descriptor costs ~90 ms/norm in eager host construction, and creating fresh semaphore/output state inside a trace fails with "Writes are not supported during trace capture". [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Diffulex-inspired two-stage vocabulary reduction** — refuted 2026-07-16; best two-stage row 35.1693 ms vs 28.3808 ms full-width (+23.9% slower) AND entropy not byte-identical (max abs delta 0.203125, mean 0.039354) because chunk partial sums reassociate the BF16 reduction. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **Compact ragged denoise MoE with the `fast_reduce` combine** — refuted 2026-07-16; +38.6% @48 but committed match 0.90625 against the >0.95 gate, because compact primary gate/up used K-block 22 instead of 8 and `fast_reduce_nc` rounds each route contribution before summation while the baseline combine matmul fuses multiply-accumulate. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **"The manual GQA fallback dominates the denoise attention"** — refuted 2026-07-16; `probe_sdpa_fallback_frequency.py` measured 0/30 fallback layers on a short canonical prompt and on a 2013-token logical / 2016-token cache prefix — native SDPA is the live path and the claim came from a June bring-up allocator state. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **`topk` k=1 / k=32 as the argmax replacement** — refuted (dg-08 audit); both measure 42.5 ms on TILE input, slower than converting the input to ROW_MAJOR (14.4 ms). [work_log.md](optimize_perf/work_log.md)
- **`chunked_entropy` (L1-resident vocab-chunked entropy)** — refuted (dg-08 audit); 45.43 ms vs the 43.06 ms baseline, because the extra slice/op launches (2 passes × 8 chunks) outweigh the DRAM-write saving. [work_log.md](optimize_perf/work_log.md)
- **L1 placement of the accept entropy tensor** — refuted (dg-08 audit); 0.345 ms vs 0.321 ms in DRAM, because resharding a one-tile-row tensor costs more than it saves. [work_log.md](optimize_perf/work_log.md)
- **Three hypotheses for the second-request GPQA prefill hang** — refuted 2026-07-22; direct wrapper A→B with early halt passed at K=17/19, replaying all skipped windows after a K=13 halt still hung, and fixed-K capture hung identically, so it was neither a request-reset bug, nor incomplete trace/CCL advancement, nor early-halt-specific — warming the 160-token prefill shape fixed it. [work_log.md](optimize_perf/work_log.md)
- **The stacked-lever route to 30 t/s at the full 48-step budget** — refuted; the arithmetic dense 1.10 → +true-sparse 5.15 → +commit batching 11.28 → +24 steps 18.83 → +2CQ/dedup 21.65 t/s stops short of 30, and two of its stacked levers ran on paths later deleted (`path_to_30tps.md`, deleted 2026-07-30). [path_to_100tps.md](optimize_perf/path_to_100tps.md)
- **"The precision-neutral in-repo speed levers are exhausted at ~17.8–18.2 t/s"** — superseded twice; the bounded 1024-row sliding read alone is −18.9% s/block and the full-canvas RMSNorm shipped as the only path on 2026-07-30. [optimize_perf/README.md](optimize_perf/README.md)
- **The five-lever "~1.08 → ~18 t/s" landed-lever claim** — provenance only (`landed_levers_47465_comment.md`, deleted 2026-07-30); four of its five levers describe code that has since been deleted, and its stated in-repo @48 ceiling was superseded twice. [perf_progress.md](optimize_perf/perf_progress.md)
- **The host IID Gumbel arm as the language-drift cure** — refuted 2026-07-28 and DELETED; it drifts on exactly the same prompts as `device`, repairs 0, and costs 1.40× per request. [optimize_perf/README.md](optimize_perf/README.md)
- **Transferring GPT-OSS router and CCL precision policies** — refuted and removed; the layer-19 DRAM→L1 CCL buffer normalization is a no-op on DG's already tile-aligned 704-wide TP shard, and GPT-OSS HiFi3 / fp32-dest / numeric-stable router softmax left step-0 argmax unchanged while *lowering* entropy PCC `0.97713 → 0.97267`. GPT-OSS also validates only shallow per-component PCC and uses incompatible router and residual/norm semantics, so its precision policy does not transfer. *(Ported 2026-07-30 from the root `AUTOFIX.md` before deleting it.)*
- **A separate FP32 residual accumulator across layers 0–20 with BF16 branch compute** — refuted and removed; step 0 improved (`argmax 0.91797 → 0.92188`, entropy PCC `0.97713 → 0.98733`) but the seeded eight-step gate *failed* (committed match `0.89453`, minimum entropy PCC `0.03358`). Another instance of the recurring trap: a one-step accuracy gain that moves the diffusion feedback trajectory the wrong way. *(Ported 2026-07-30 from the root `AUTOFIX.md`.)*

## Kernels, rooflines and op profiles

- **Pinning the MoE gather and down matmul outputs to L1 (`DG_MOE_L1`)** — refuted; isolated MoE fwd −3.2% but traced e2e −0.6% @48 / +0.4% @12 at bit-identical `committed_sha`, because those DRAM writes already overlap adjacent compute under trace. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **Gate/up activation L1 (MED-5)** — refuted; a no-op by construction, `batched_experts` is weight-bound at M=1 tile (62% of the MoE) and does not move, so the MoE is weight-traffic-bound not activation-round-trip-bound. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **Residual-stream L1 (HIGH-3)** — refuted; every consumer of the 256x2816 residual takes a DRAM-interleaved input, so pinning it alone only inserts a reshard at each boundary (OPT-003 residual-contract rule: it pays only as a whole-layer L1 stack). [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **Attention activation L1 (MED-6)** — refuted; the `to_memory_config(..., DRAM)` force at `diffusion_attention.py:400-411` is a guarded passthrough no-op, and a real L1-sharded SDPA is blocked by the flash-SDPA CB clash. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **Mask L1 (MED-7)** — refuted; the disp/comb/disp_t masks are ~2 MB and the ops sub-ms, so there is no material headroom. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **Layout conversion as the denoise lever** — refuted; ALL `InterleavedToSharded` + `ShardedToInterleaved` device-FW is ~1.34 ms over 6 layers (~4 ms/step at 30L, <3% of the step). [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **The `DG_NORM_FULLCANVAS` flip gate's `committed_match = 0.145` FAIL** — refuted 2026-07-30; both arms ran on the token-gather MoE deleted in `7417bd7d69d` for not converging, so the gate compared two arms sitting on a broken baseline. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **`bench_norm_fullcanvas.py`'s "PCC 0.999998 / ~2e-6 per norm"** — refuted 2026-07-30; it computed PCC as an fp32 dot product over ~720K elements and reported values above 1.0 in its own table, so its resolution floor is ~5e-5. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)
- **The "27% shorter answers" objection to the full-canvas norm** — refuted; a 10-question artifact, −10% at 71 questions and gone at 198. [l1_residency.md](optimize_perf/l1_residency.md)

[whole_gen_opprofile/README.md](optimize_perf/whole_gen_opprofile/README.md)
- **The 42.1% cumsum `PermuteDeviceOperation` as an op-level lever** — refuted 2026-07-06; a sum-of-device-FW overlap artifact worth ~1% of real time (eager `cumsum(dim=2)` 0.178 ms against one expert matmul at 4.17 ms). [whole_gen_opprofile/README.md](optimize_perf/whole_gen_opprofile/README.md)
