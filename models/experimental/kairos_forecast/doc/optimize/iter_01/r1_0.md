| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| probe move (one row, 1.3x or 0.7x) | 0.3 |
| bound | 0.15 |
| fixed record against itself | 0 |

**Decision: precision, not a defect.**
- The embedding and all six encoder blocks are ordinary for this series in both halves (rank 9-154 of 256). A
  routing, patching or masking defect would show from the embedding or encoder on.
- The series becomes the worst row from decoder block 2 on, while the other rows' decoder error falls. Then the final
  decoder RMSNorm multiplies its relative error by about 3.1x in both presets (acc 0.018 -> 0.057, perf 0.079 ->
  0.25). The other rows grow by 1.5x (p50) to 1.9x (p99) there. This row's decoder state is ill-conditioned under the
  final norm's weights.
- The error scales with the preset (perf/acc about 5x at every tap). It is odd in the input: the head error is
  -0.060 on x and +0.066 on -x. So the flip average (a - flip(b)) / 2 adds the halves instead of cancelling them,
  as the D5 review read it.
- Follow-up (a candidate, not a fix): fp32 for the decoder residual stream / final-norm input on the accuracy preset.
  That would be a precision change, with the v1.14 table.
