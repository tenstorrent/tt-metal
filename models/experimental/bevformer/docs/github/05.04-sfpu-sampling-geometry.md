## Context

Fused MSDA dominates layer kernel time, and #55199 assumed that cost was either inherent to the sampling work or general kernel overhead. It is neither.

A profiling branch measured `CB-COMPUTE-WAIT-FRONT` at **36.1 ms on a 36.0 ms call** — the compute kernel idle for the entire op, waiting on the reader. Zones put 20.3 ms of that in the coordinate arithmetic, at ~140 cycles per float operation: soft-float emulation on a dataflow core with no FPU, while the SFPU sat unused. Moving the arithmetic to the vector unit took the op from **167.6 ms to 29.5 ms**, sampling kernel untouched.

N150 figures from `ctr-ikasic/bev_former_kernel_optimizations`. Evidence for the proposal, not a Blackhole result and not the current implementation.

## Problem

- The reader derives per-point geometry — pixel-space normalization, `floor`, bilinear fractions, the four corner weights — in float, on a core with no FPU.
- The compute kernel cannot start until the reader delivers, so the op measures as reader latency.
- This also explains #55199's "~45% slower per sample than bare `grid_sample`": that compared the soft-float reader, not the fusion.

## Proposed direction

- Derive `px`, `x0`, `dx` and the four corner weights (already multiplied by `attn`) on the SFPU.
- Hand the reader `x0`/`y0` as bf16; it decodes with integer shifts, bounds-tests, and turns them into page indices — no float left in the reader.
- Carry the `align_corners` variant in the `scale`/`shift` constants so the kernel stays branch-free.
- Enable `fp32_dest_acc_en`.

Two defects surfaced on the profiling branch when the arithmetic moved, both to be covered by tests rather than rediscovered:

- **Out-of-bounds corners lose their mask.** The reader used to fold the bounds test into the scalar it built. A compute kernel that builds that scalar knows nothing about bounds, so stale rows get multiplied by live weights — a high-error ratio near 0.55 that the PCC gate was loose enough to pass, in every configuration.
- **`fp32_dest_acc_en` is required, and is faster.** bf16's ulp at 200 is 1.0, so `floor(px)` on a 200x200 map rounds wrong and bilinear degrades to nearest-neighbour. PCC there: 0.996156 without, 0.999823 with; the op went 35.9 to 29.5 ms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Geometry runs on the SFPU; the reader performs no float arithmetic.
- [ ] `CB-COMPUTE-WAIT-FRONT` is no longer the dominant term, reported before and after.
- [ ] `fp32_dest_acc_en` is enabled, with its PCC and device-time effects reported separately.
- [ ] Out-of-bounds corners are masked, with a test that fails without it; `expected_high_error_ratio` is asserted, not just PCC.
- [ ] Feature-map size limits implied by bf16 coordinates are asserted in the kernel.
- [ ] Fused-op unit tests and BEVFormer MSDA/TSA/SCA/layer/encoder PCC suites pass at their configured thresholds, including PCC >= 0.997 for the base encoder.
- [ ] Blackhole device time is measured directly; the N150 figures are not carried over as results.
