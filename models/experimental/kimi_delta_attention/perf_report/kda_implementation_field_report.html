Matmul + reduce-scatter

The row-parallel output GEMM overlaps a Ring reduce-scatter. An 8×8 matmul grid leaves rows 8–9 available; four sender workers begin at (0,8).

The scan is the guide’s recurrence, factored

Vnew = T⁻¹ · (vβ − kd · Sin) O = qd · Sin + Aqk · Vnew Sout = Diag(dl) · Sin + kdecᵀ · Vnew

The correction term is evaluated against the decayed incoming state, matching the token-ordered reference.

Five guide-to-source corrections

Projection: retained prefill uses one fused GEMM, not independent Q/K/V linears.
Layout: aligned Q/K/V stay flat; the prep reader addresses heads and chunks directly.
β dtype: sigmoid emits BF16; chunk_kda converts it to FP32 at the recurrence boundary.
Prepared storage: arithmetic stays FP32, but the selected prep→scan boundary stores kd, q_decay, and dl in BF16. Sensitive v_beta, k_dec_t, intra, t_inv, state, and outputs remain FP32.
Gate storage: aligned chunk prefill stores the transformed decay gate in BF16; recurrent/decode and all gate-consuming arithmetic remain FP32.
Routing: there is no automatic T threshold. The caller selects mode; fused recurrent accepts exactly T=1.

04 · roofline diagnosis

Mixed storage moves the memory roof; compute remains far away and CCL is essentially there

Blackhole HiFi4 peak is 152.064 TFLOP/s per chip; DRAM is 512 GB/s, giving a 297 FLOP/byte ridge. The whole-layer compute percentage uses the 8-chip mesh peak and executed FLOPs. CCL uses the effective two-link payload roofline.

Kernel arithmetic-intensity roofline

Prep and scan sit far left of the ridge: more matrix-FPU peak does not move their roof. The useful levers are bytes, layout programs, state traffic, and parallel scheduling.

Fresh whole-layer target utilization

Interpretation. At T=5,120, CCL is 39.79% against the 40% aspiration—within 0.21 percentage points. Compute is 12.40% against 60%, but that does not mean the layer is simply an inefficient GEMM: recurrence prep/scan are DRAM-bound, output is communication-bound, and the ordered scan cannot fan out across chunks.
Distribution consequence. Preserve the 8×8 output matmul grid and two RS rows. Attempts to donate matmul cores to CCL made the fused boundary slower. On recurrence, keep 16 scan cores at H=4; adding synchronization to share reads also lost badly.

Compute target

Executed work scales 8× from 67.594 to 540.752 GFLOP, while wall grows 5.57×. Utilization rises from 8.63% to 12.40%, so longer sequence amortizes fixed/layout overhead but does not change the memory-bound recurrence roof.

CCL target

Effective two-link utilization rises from 35.05% to 39.79%. The T=5,120 output boundary is the closest part of the layer to its aspirational target.

Serial axis

Prep grows from 80 independent items to 640 and fills all 110 cores. Scan stays at 16 workers, each walking 20→160 chunks; its latency grows almost linearly with sequence.
