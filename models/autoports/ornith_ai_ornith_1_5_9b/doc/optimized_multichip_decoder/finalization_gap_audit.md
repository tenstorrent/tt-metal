## What is already supported

The default is actual TP4, not four copies of a decoder. `tt/multichip_decoder.py:1225–1280` groups dense projections and independently handles request attention/cache states. `decode_finish:697–704` performs two intra-layer row-parallel reductions per decoder. The layer boundary carries replicated-across-ranks BF16 `[1,1,B,4096]`, width-sharded locally on 32 cores with `(32,128)` shards. The next layer's `to_memory_config(..., ar_mem)` is a same-config operation when this contract is honored. The packed stack avoids host unpack and boundary CCL. README's inter-layer contract is usable; retain it and distinguish mesh replication of the residual from model replication.

CCL is already async and receives setup-owned ping-pong buffers (`MultichipCollectives:68–97`, `decode_reduce:428–443`). Four ARs cost64.3635 us in the target two-kind profile. Separate workspaces, changing inputs, cache on/off, semaphore-bank reuse and watcher have evidence after the full-grid initialization repair (`history_audit/AUTOFIX.md`, `ccl_grid_fix_jobs.json`, `ccl_grid_stress_jobs.json`). Do not label async CCL or persistent receive buffers untried.

Coherent hidden-residual families were tried: `tests/multichip_batched_family_candidate.py:36–68,71–120` keeps1024 columns/rank across layers and distributed norms. `batched_family_*_32_retry.json` records separate1.344417 ms, persistent-RS1.329495, fused gather2.349413, scatter2.396023, both3.393444 against then-default1.049989.
