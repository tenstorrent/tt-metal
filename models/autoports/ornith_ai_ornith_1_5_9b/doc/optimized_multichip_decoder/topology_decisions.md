# Operation-topology decisions

This supplements the **pre-candidate audit** at the start of work_log.md. These
are measured candidate decisions. The final default is integrated; all 82 final jobs, profiles and the consolidated
audit pass. Independent review remains pending. All mesh results below use TP4 local
heads/sharded weights, not replicated decoder execution. Times are serial,
traced warmed two-kind packed-stack latency unless explicitly marked prefill.

The historical cumulative candidate (pool-B1, fast-zero, conv/gate fusion,
BF8 AR payload, HiFi2/FP32-destination SDPA K64) passed B1/B32 short,64K128steps and
4K1024steps:0.483731/0.587295/3.634062/0.828453ms. These numbers are candidate
results, not substitutes for final-default measurements. The final path must
be measured again after the capacity-aware SDPA repair. See
`context128k_autofix/AUTOFIX.md` for the controlled failure and repair matrix.

The model is dense; active-expert/MoE tests do not apply. Full-model LM head,
sampling, generation quality and vLLM are outside this decoder stage.
