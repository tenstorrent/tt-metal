### Performance accounting (same run)

Source: `doc/optimized_decoder/perf/accounting.json`.

| workload | roofline ms | device ms | end-to-end ms | device/roofline | e2e-device (host) ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| traced warmed decode, batch 1, context 4096, sliding_rope | 0.536 | 1.050 | 1.064 | 1.96x | 0.014 |
| traced warmed decode, batch 1, context 4096, full_nope | 0.536 | 1.035 | 1.045 | 1.93x | 0.010 |
| warmed prefill, 8192 tokens, sliding_rope | 13.035 | 51.041 | 54.812 | 3.92x | 3.771 |
| warmed prefill, 8192 tokens, full_nope | 13.035 | 48.911 | 53.542 | 3.75x | 4.631 |

Named limitations:

* Decode device time is 1.96x the DRAM roofline. `MatmulMultiCoreReuseMultiCastDRAMSharded` fixes its worker set to one core per DRAM bank (12 on this p300c), and the committed rows show those workers simultaneously at ~53% of their DRAM share and ~53% of their LoFi FLOP share - neither is saturated, so the gap is DRAM-read/compute overlap on a 12-core worker set, not a missing knob. A wider worker set is not expressible: the op derives its workers itself, and the alternative 104-core interleaved family measured 32% slower at the same dtype (see the candidate table).
* Decode end-to-end is only 1.3% above device time (1.0641 ms vs 1.0505 ms), i.e. the traced decode loop carries no material host term.
* Prefill matmuls run on 64 of 110 cores: `grid_x` is pinned to the 8 DRAM banks because a 2D multicast matmul with DRAM width-sharded weights returns NaN for any other width (scripts/repro_prefill_matmul_grid_x9.py), and `grid_y` above 8 measured 48% slower. Against the 64-core LoFi peak the prefill matmul roofline is 22.4 ms and the measured matmul time is 33.4 ms (67%); the reported 13.0 ms roofline is the unreachable 110-core figure.
* Prefill end-to-end is 7-9% above device time (54.81 ms vs 51.04 ms at 8192 tokens for sliding_rope), down from ~20% in the functional stage. What remains is per-chunk host dispatch; prefill is not traced because the chunk count depends on the prompt length.
* Non-matmul prefill time is 34% of the window: SDPA 13.1%, elementwise 9.3%, norms 8.5%. Prefill activations are DRAM interleaved by design, so the norms are not sharded.
