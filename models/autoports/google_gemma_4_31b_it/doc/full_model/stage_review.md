- **Scalar stochastic settings affected only slot 0.** Evidence: the actual common formatter treated scalar temperature as one active lane, padding the other lanes as greedy; the initial state tests discarded sampled outputs. Affected path: batched stochastic generation. Control and investigation: reviewer reproduced the formatter behavior on host, inspected `set_sampling` broadcasting/validation, and ran `tests.check_pool_admission` successfully. Final tracked B1/8/16/32 tests compare seeded sequences in every active slot and retain the outputs. Resolution: repaired and validated; common sampler normalization remains in use.

- **Repeated mixed prefill produced different logits.** Evidence: `AUTODEBUG_REPEAT.md` and the sampler-disabled baseline probe showed differences up to 33.3125, while frozen-logit sampling remained stable. Affected path: wrapper embedding all-gather during batched prefill. Control and investigation: adding only its missing device barrier produced 11 bitwise-equal comparisons in `prefill_repeat_probe_barrier.json`; stage observations that masked the failure were not retained as a fix. Reviewer inspected the CCL factory/kernel barrier predicate and the inherited decoder gather contract. Final tracked state tests and full-stack 32-slot logit equality pass. Resolution: repaired by the device barrier, with no host wait or precision/layout change. The exact allocator/lifetime race explanation remains an inference; the A/B effect is measured.

- **Thermodynamics wording was factually weaker than the original HF branch.** Evidence: shared prompt 3 says energy in a "closed system" remains constant, where the original HF continuation says "isolated system". Affected path: free-running qualitative text after earlier branching. Control and investigation: pinned HF was conditioned on the exact TT prefix before the disputed token; `thermodynamics_control.json` ranks the TT token " closed" first at 24.125 versus 9.8125 for the runner-up and reproduces the statement. Both controls and generated texts were read. Resolution: expected HF behavior on the branched prefix, not an unexplained TT-specific discrepancy; this does not make the statement factually correct.

Affected path: reference stopping. Control and investigation: deterministic prefixes are retained through the first pinned stop token, with original outputs preserved; AIME100 is unaffected. Resolution: controlled, with matching TT stop handling and final qualitative records.

- **Late preparation allocations conflicted with live traces.** Evidence: `AUTODEBUG.md`, `AUTOFIX.md`, original trace failure and subsequent tracked runs. Control and investigation: reset, all physical tail buckets and both terminal modes are warmed before capture; fixed page views and runtime offsets avoid new request-specific program signatures. Control and investigation: separate final-source clean runs record all diagnostic flags as zero and preserve steady-state counters.
