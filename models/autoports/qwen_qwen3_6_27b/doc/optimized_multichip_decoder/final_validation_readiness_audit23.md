### B1 prefill regression: independent paired process measurements, zero margin

The timed prefill body is unchanged: three warmed eager calls, with reset/synchronization outside the timed call as before. `run_multichip_decoder.py` now records those three samples and explicitly identifies their arithmetic mean. These samples verify the run-level estimator; they are **not three independent statistical replicates**.

The final checker requires at least five independent control/final process pairs for each of layers0 and3 at B1S512. Arm order must alternate control/final then final/control. Result files cannot be reused; command start times must follow the declared sequential order. Each pair must share the exact current harness SHA, reference metadata, native source/build and instrumentation regime. Commands may differ only by output destination and the pinned control-source argument; diagnostics, policy overrides, legacy decode and instrumented timing cannot make a slower control. Final arms must be the current default path.

The explicit method is `exact_paired_median_upper_bound`: form each independent run pair's final-minus-control difference of the three-call mean, then use the smallest order statistic whose Binomial(n,0.5) upper tail is at most0.05. This is a distribution-free, one-sided at-least95% upper confidence bound for the **population median paired run-mean difference**. At n=5 the bound is the largest difference, with96.875% coverage. The bound must be <=0ms: there is no relaxed percentage or additive margin. This deliberately does not claim a confidence bound on the population arithmetic mean. The computed median, bound, sample count, order statistic and achieved coverage are written into the final gate result, including a failing bound.

The control is the immutable accepted TP4 implementation from commit`8502f42bca20`, SHA`082c82faa6b9500f654dcc3f0e26f1c4fab656e987d3fd079bc1c79ec19a3c44`.
