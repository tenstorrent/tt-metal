CPU/source audit, 2026-11-13. No TTNN/Torch import, device access, build, or production edits. This is a proposal with evidence coverage, not a new performance or completion claim. The two measured profiles are TP4 on the target 1×4 P300c mesh, B32/S513, decoder source `430dd1278b9f8cf031e7e80a147de15943e7868ba6e2c4882411f1cd4ddaf597`. The numerical reference is the accepted frozen TTNN decoder; separate HF-contract gates are unchanged.

[Advice coverage CSV](long_prefill_advice26.csv) accounts for **all 50 matmul advice entries** in both reports, including entries marked Optimized, and both high-gap groups. ## Covered experiments and precise gaps

- `prefill_chunks7`: 376 passing configurations across M64/128/256/1024/4096, genuine captured text activations, BF16 activation/BFP8 weight, both interleaved and DRAM-sharded weights where supported. Joint output stays FP32. At M4096 inherited joint 8×8 costs ~2391 us; tuned 8×4 ~6734–6795 us, adapted 1D-in0 ~9968 us, minimal ~5627–7039 us. QKVG inherited cap512 costs ~1258–1281 us; tested alternatives ~2713–3844 us. These reject those **specific** alternatives, not a ten-row-grid family or BF16 joint output. They measure projection, not joint combine/concat. Their input-L1 alternative is not established.
- `prefill5` actual B32/S513 covers caps512/2048/4096, joint grid8×8, temporal chunks128/512/1024. Its full-attention cap4096 failure records 1,608,640 bytes required versus 1,572,864 available L1; subsequent later adaptations are already recorded. Do not reclassify this as a new rejection or extrapolate it to different grids/caps/consumers. No targeted TP4 joint8×10 cap5120/2560/1280/640 or packed-QKVG7×10 cap640/1280 evidence was found. The repository's single-chip baseline `matmul_row_cap=640` is not that experiment.
- `mmrs2` already tries DRAM chunks128/512/2048/16384 at **both kinds B32/S513**. Whole prefill L0: 65.935/55.326/53.221/50.877 ms; L3: 51.987/40.724/38.436/36.429 ms. It supports the large-chunk family, using older source/policy. Do not rerun this entire sweep as untried. `advice5_mmrs_input_l1_c{512,2048}` is S33 only; a matched **long** L1-chunk family remains.
- `advice5_prefill_minimal{,_k8}` is S33 generic-projection routing; it does not establish the current fused-SwiGLU M16384 or M32 tail optimum. Decode minimal experiments likewise do not test a prefill-tail-only replacement while keeping the fast bulk route.
- `family2_gather*` long cases use `gather_norm=decode`, and current native fused-norm whole-layer trials are decode scoped. Neither measures gather-first normalization on B32 prefill. The current bulk performs stats AG plus postnorm plus activation AG twice per layer. A coherent `gather_norm=True, flat_gather_prefill=True` comparison (using the subsequently added explicit flat-admission hook) removes stats CCL and distributed norm while retaining the compact inter-layer residual; no immediate replicated residual restore is appropriate.
- Higher-fidelity BF4 retries and their KV failures are already documented; they do not measure LoFi→HiFi2 BF8 projection at this long shape. Report accuracy advice can be tested with `attention_fidelity=HiFi2` on both kinds, retaining gates. This also changes attention MMRS, so record that scope. Do not claim a fidelity speed gain before measuring it.
- Tracing advice **has been tried at this exact shape**: `traced_prefill23_l{0,3}_b32_s513_{quality,perf}.json` pass. Seven uninstrumented matched forward+output-copy samples: L0 eager44.179652→trace43.057979 ms; L3 35.419489→35.716356 ms. The profile's estimated 615 us/3 us gap advice is therefore covered. Preserve exact replay/changed-input evidence and recheck the final selected path; no runtime-argument rewrite follows automatically from that advice.

## Next bounded matrix, in order

1. **Joint projection and combine as one boundary.** First control FP32 8×8/c4096 versus its direct-output combine-window counterpart; then FP32 8×10/c5120 and c2560 with the same window. Current source now contains `prefill_direct_output`; device evidence is not established by this audit. The window eliminates per-piece output/concat and owns one final BF16 `[1,16384,4160]` result. It retains input slices and joint math. Never compare only matmul and assume concat savings.
2. Consume the already-prepared `joint_output23` long **prefill-only BF16 output** case before duplicating it. Its 8×8/c4096, FP32-DST arithmetic is separate from reduced accumulation. If quality holds, compare BF16-pack/FP32-acc 8×10/c5120, then same BF16 output on 7×10 with FP32-acc/subblock1×4 versus BF16-acc/subblock1×8. **Current window admission explicitly requires FP32 joint output** (`packed_correction_candidate.py:162`): do not enable it with BF16 and call that first API error a rejection. The BF16 family currently uses its valid direct BF16-input combine and concat; a writer-format adaptation is required before composing the two winners. No full-tensor FP32 restore and no silent gate-precision change.
3. **Norm/AG placement**, both kinds: gather-first local norm vs existing distributed norm through the next projection, same compact residual output. This addresses ~6.1 ms AG plus ~0.79 ms distributed norm, although most activation AG traffic remains. Compare actual downstream layout and transfers, not an isolated norm.
4. **Full-attention QKVG movement/geometry**, 7×10/c640 and c1280 against current cap512; include output concat, split/transpose, Q/K norms, RoPE and gate consumer. Compose with the separately prepared direct-prefill-QKV movement helper only after its standalone/whole-layer evidence. The ~2.7 ms slice family is material and overlaps other operations: do not attribute all of it to QKVG alone.
5. On winning projection geometries try input-L1 (joint c2560 first; QKVG bounded cap) and HiFi2. Include DRAM→L1 copies and capacity under the live layer. If capacity fails, reduce cap and record total boundary rather than dropping L1 after the first error.
6. **MMRS**: current DRAM c16384 vs L1 c2048, then c512 if needed, both attention-out K1536 and down K4352, all N5120, including copies/concat. Native API has no `program_config`: it exposes `l1_headroom`, `output_tensor`, compute config and placement (`.../matmul_reduce_scatter_prefill.hpp:26`). Thus report generic subblock advice is not a missing Python kwarg. Record planner worker/output rows/subblocks for the actual call; if planner utilization remains poor, bounded headroom/output-buffer adaptation is the legal next experiment. Preserve output width1280 per rank and no inter-layer CCL.
7. **Fused SwiGLU**: bulk config M4/K4/N16, grid11×10, subblock1×4 is an explicit control despite generic report wording. Try K8 and bounded L1 chunks with fused epilogue retained; current production route needs a scoped config adapter, since `minimal_blocks` changes a different generic route. Separately replace only M32 prefill-tail work by the existing packed gate/up+fused SwiGLU family. Keeping bulk minimal avoids hiding a tail benefit behind a bulk regression. Both need matched actual whole-layer timing.

## Geometry, ownership and validity proof

Joint physical N=7168 gives 224 tile columns. Grid8×10 has per-core N28; cap5120 has per-core M16 and out-block H4, exactly the old cap4096/grid8×8 M16/N28/H4 but **80 workers instead of64**. Actual M16384 becomes 5120+5120+5120+1024 (four calls, not three); tail per-core M4. Cap2560 is six full chunks+1024; cap1280 is twelve+1024; cap640 is twenty-five+384, so launch overhead makes the latter two contingent, low-priority tests. For grid7×10, N32 permits a real eight-wide subblock only with the explicit reduced-DST choice. BF16 output alone does not authorize it.

QKVG physical N3584 gives112 tile columns; grid7×10 gives N16. Cap640 gives M2, cap1280 M4; their M16384 tails are384 and1024 respectively. Existing `_linear_raw` supports per-key grid/block/input-L1 but cap is global `matmul_row_cap`; record that policy scope. The JSON preserves FP32 accumulation in these movement/geometry proposals.

CPU-only calls of the lazy-import `prefill_plan` verify all proposed internal chunks across B1..32 and S1/3/31/32/33/127/511/512/513: 9,104 chunk plans, padded row coverage, K divisibility, N coverage and DST limits. This proves descriptor legality only, **not L1 fit, native acceptance, numerical quality or speed**. Keep public unaligned lengths; pad only internal rows and slice owned outputs. Window buffers must remain invocation-owned and trace/preallocation ownership must be rechecked when composed. Run unchanged output, contribution, per-user, state/cache PCC gates; preserve watcher/tracker separation and save rejected candidates' exact before/after evidence. Final performance must come from the selected default; none of these proposed numbers satisfy the stage Stop Rule.
