# Final28 advice preparation

CPU-only historical evidence reconciliation; final profiles are not yet used or
claimed covered. `final28_advice.json` is the exact list schema accepted by
`final28_evidence.py assemble --advice`. Its five exact Unicode Advice strings
cover all141 nonempty historical CSV occurrences found under `perf/`, including
the long-prefill25, fusions20, integrated14 and precision-geometry5 profiles.
`final28_advice_inventory.json` retains source CSV, row ID, operation geometry,
fidelity/dtypes and worker count for each occurrence. Final profile strings
must still be compared; an unknown string remains a missing disposition.

One obsolete512×5120×4608 projection advisory is not applicable because the
selected joint projection has physical N7168. This exception applies only to
that historical geometry. Reappearance of the same text for another operation
must be reviewed against its actual geometry.

The other four aggregate strings are conservatively classified
`not_tried_stop_rule` because each still has an applicable unmeasured scope.
Their reasons separately preserve measured rejections/kept choices:

- Joint FP32 and packed-QKVG exact-geometry projection alternatives from
  prefill_chunks7 were measured; long input-L1/fidelity and new coherent
  ten-row-grid/consumer families were not.
- MMRS DRAM chunk128/512/2048/16384 was measured at B32/S513 and supports the
  selected large chunks. S33 L1-placement and minimal/K8 configurations also
  passed and were measured; they cannot discharge long fused-SwiGLU bulk/tail
  or long MMRS input-L1 work.
- The90/110 grid advice is native MMRS, which exposes no public grid or
  program_config argument. A legal planner/headroom/output-row adaptation
  remains unrun; no blanket core-count or first-API-error rejection is made.

Thus the four aggregate labels do **not** mean the historical advice was
ignored, and do not turn a prepared matrix into measured evidence. Each cites
actual tested artifacts plus the exact user wrap-up record and capped handoff.
The underlying separate reasons are available for per-operation reviewer
inspection even though the assembler keys advice by exact string alone.
All referenced files exist. No device work, production source changes,
quality relaxation, final profile pass or stage-review claim was made.
