## Limitations and final acceptance navigation

- [final29_handoff.md](final29_handoff.md) enumerates untried capped manifests and remaining coherent adaptations. Their absence is neither a numerical rejection nor evidence of speed. Full-model/MoE work is out of scope.
- [final28_selection.md](final28_selection.md) maps selected policies to measured candidate rows. The original untouched-path measurements and all numbers above are historical; rerun selected defaults for final reported values. Fresh same-current-harness B1 prefill zero-margin nonregression is inconclusive for both kinds as quantified above; it is not resolved by faster point estimates.
- Precision .995 output/contribution/per-user/state gates, logical nonaligned lengths, changed-input traces, watcher/tracker checks, B32 long stress,64-owner resources, context and runtime fallback audit must remain unchanged. Candidate family evidence cannot replace cumulative selected-path receipts. `final29_runtime_audit.json` passes static coverage/48 mutation controls and attaches passing final-default runtime sentinels for both kinds. Scope is limited to its documented six public transfer APIs during initial eager prefill/decode and capture; it does not certify arbitrary Torch calls or every timed forward. See `final29_runtime_scope.md`.
- Carry residual `[1,B,1280]` BF16/rank through decoder boundaries. Any future inter-layer collective or conversion requires a coherent measured benefit; inside-layer AG/RS is legitimate and must remain distinguishable in instrumentation.
- Original strict stop criterion (≥70% whole-layer roofline plus last two kept<3%, or8h occupied device time) was not established by these records. The explicit subsequent cap in `user_wrapup28.json` / `user_wrapup28_runner.txt` ends new search. Native matmul bandwidth or an isolated fusion percentage is not whole-layer roofline. No stage-review verdict, final evidence checker pass or local commit is asserted here.
