## Frozen control

All decode, requested-decode, and capture batches are 32. The model config and
decoder construction both give 48 `linear_attention` plus 16
`full_attention` layers, summing to `num_hidden_layers=64`.

Timing used the repository `scripts/harness_template.py` protocol through the
model hooks in `harness.py`, in a fresh process per configuration. Full
attention used 10 untimed warmups and five blocks of 50 trace replays:
median 1.449416 ms, spread 0.749 µs. Linear attention was remeasured with 30
untimed warmups after the initial settling advisory: median 15.852625 ms,
spread 5.795 µs. Its feasibility remains `not_measurable` even if the first
repeat is excluded because its advisor-drop ceiling is exactly zero.

The incumbent was frozen before both captures. The pinned advisor binary is
from `618cd4e75dae69d334bb9d8cdeff314816ccf214`. Captures used the executed
`candidate=default` policy: BF16 full-attention weights, BFP8 KV cache, BFP4
MLP; and BFP4 linear input/output, BFP8 recurrent state, BFP4 MLP.

## Reach and reconciliation

Full attention captured 65 ops with two spills. Its one-replay profile window
is 1,267.432 µs; reconciliation closes at 100% and is not degraded. The
advisor-drop ceiling is 33.698 µs/layer, 44.99× the 0.749 µs control
spread, so the ten ranked attributable chains were screened.

The stateful gated-delta token mixer is terminal in the pinned tracer at
`ttnn.copy`, `softplus`, and recurrent-state update operations. The shipped
outer residual/norm/MLP envelope was therefore captured and the terminal
region declared `uncapturable` in the report. Reconciliation against the full
15,833.007 µs linear-layer profile closes at 100%, is not degraded, and
records 63.50% as expected untraced time. In the reachable envelope the
advisor drops **0.000 µs** of boundaries, below the 5.795 µs spread, so the
`not_measurable` verdict forbids screening this kind. Its contribution is zero
by the explicit arithmetic, not by candidate suppression.

Both reconciliation JSON files are generated by `scripts/reconcile.py`. A
separate results overlay supplies device measurements and exact hard errors;
the accounting and ranking are never hand-authored.

- `advisor_head_norm_l1` (`:b12`): height-sharded layernorm inputs unsupported.

All losing controls remain default-off and are named in `final.json`.
