## Measured Stop Rule

Rule 2 applies: the last two kept candidates each gained less than 3% on primary single-user TPOT-derived decode rate. These cumulative measurements all use 128 input /128 output /1 request, concurrency 1, random prompts, explicit temperature 0, ignore_eos, all 64 layers, max_num_seqs 32, max_model_len 262144, P300x2 TP4, scheduler budget 512, async scheduling, trace/device-sampling all, selected datatype, tracking 0 and telemetry 0. Exact server command equality and benchmark configuration equality were checked when constructing S10-K. The CI workload is 100/100/32 with **no explicit concurrency cap**, not a forced client concurrency 32 row.

| Cumulative kept candidate | Previous candidate | Primary mean TPOT ms | Primary 1000/TPOT t/s/u | Adjacent t/s/u gain |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| C2: page maximum hoist | C1 | 26.573463676747 | 37.631526404104 | −0.000921172748% |
| C3: page diagnostic telemetry guard | C2 | 26.572559824521 | 37.632806421502 | +0.003401449583% |

These tiny single-request differences are not statistically established speedups. C2/C3 retain simpler request-boundary host work with unchanged device contracts; C1 is retained for the paired mixed-traffic improvement and its explicitly reported latency tradeoffs. C3 primary TTFT is 277.926235 ms at 128/128/1, versus baseline 276.979049 ms at 128/128/1. CI 100/100/32 aggregate output throughput is secondary capacity evidence: baseline 229.325791→C3 228.208660 tokens/s. No headline decode win is inferred from a burst metric. Final selected-source reproduction is S10-V; S10-E/R/P closes P4/P5, runtime, cleanup and gate classification. Independent review returned clean-pass and commit_receipt.json records local closure.

## Not tried (stop rule)

These are handoff notes after rule 2, not blockers or measured rejections. Expected gains are unknown for every item:

- Shared full-batch page-table backing and eliding forced uploads of identical maps.
- Intermediate decode buckets between 1 and 32.
- Serving-safe additional prefill traces.
- Unequal-row batched prefill and optional dense-burst adoption; no batched-equals-solo claim is made for an unadopted candidate.
- Selected-policy matmul geometry, Blackhole reader counts and phase-specific activation shards.
- Further fused or sharded terminal norm, LM-head and collective alternatives.
- General non32-aligned continuation vectorization that preserves GDN arithmetic; arbitrary nonaligned inputs retain their accepted supported path.

The rejected multi-row extension and rejected global 32 partition change are measured correctness decisions documented in AUTOFIX/work-log evidence, not untried items. Final failed gates, review findings, cleanup and source-provenance issues are work regardless of this search stop.
