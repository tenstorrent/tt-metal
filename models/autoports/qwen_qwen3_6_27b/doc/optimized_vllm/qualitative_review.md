No mechanical looping, doubled-token progression, single-token collapse, gibberish, visible cross-request contamination, interior role/control-token leakage, or unexplained language switching was observed in these 12 served texts. The supplied story opening is repeated on both sides as part of the story continuation. Terminal `<|im_end|>` markers in the standalone controls arise from the collector's raw tokenizer decoding; they are absent from the served text.

There is no population-quality verdict. Under the qualitative-check skill, a relative content-quality failure claim requires at least 10 matched samples per side, blinded scoring, and the predetermined comparison rule. This run has one unseeded served sample per prompt and seed0 controls. It supports the scoped serving observations above and leaves those statistical claims unmade. Dedicated sampling, degeneracy, trace, mixed-traffic, and eval gates retain their own requirements.
