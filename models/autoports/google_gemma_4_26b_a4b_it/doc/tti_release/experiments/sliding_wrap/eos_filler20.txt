For a particle of mass $m$ in a one-dimensional box of width $L$ (from $x=0$ to $x=L$), the unperturbed energy eigenfunctions are:
$$\psi_n(x) = \sqrt{\frac{2}{L}} \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$$
For the ground state ($n=1$):
$$\psi_1(x) = \sqrt{\frac{2}{L}} \sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right)$$

$$E_1^{(1)} = \langle \psi_1 | V(x) | \psi_1 \rangle = \int_{0}^{L} \psi_1^*(x) V(x) \psi_1(x) \, dx$$
