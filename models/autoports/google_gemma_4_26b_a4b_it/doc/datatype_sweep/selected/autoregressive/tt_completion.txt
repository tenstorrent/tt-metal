Imagine you are standing outside on a sunny day. You see the blue sky, but if you look at the sun, it’s bright yellow-white. Where did all that blue come from?

To understand this, we have to look at two things: **Light** and **Air.**

### 1. Light is a Rainbow in Disguise
Even though sunlight looks white, it is actually made of all the colors of the rainbow: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet.
