Startup OpenMPI shared-memory capacity and unknown-motherboard discovery warnings were also present in successful prior-stage/device runs. They are not tensor-compute fallback. TTNN CCL deprecation warnings refer to retained accepted decoder APIs. Reduced B1/B32 profiles and sampler/terminal controls now quantify the current path (sampling_contract.md and tracy/*/trace_phase_timing.json). No controlled packet-size comparison isolates the warning's overhead; retaining the inherited transport policy is not a fallback. No hardware reset has occurred in this stage.

The common source precision policy, cache dtype split and rejection ledger remain in the accepted stage05 artifacts; no precision change is justified by this audit. Source inspection is not a clean-pass verdict.
