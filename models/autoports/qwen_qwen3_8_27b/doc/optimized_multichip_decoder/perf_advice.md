Fused RS scalar `HiFi4` is the last reduction kernel's metadata, not the matmul fidelity. Captured matmul attributes plus exact compiled bmm descriptor hashes independently prove LoFi (`kernel_fidelity_evidence/`, `runtime_contract.json`). Raw per-op percentages above100% therefore have an unsuitable scalar-fidelity denominator and are not copied into telemetry. Whole-layer rooflines use useful work, all four physical ASICs' theoretical peak and the complete layer envelope, including all operations and gaps; those calculations are in `performance.json` and `tests/report_optimized_multichip.py`.

The v5 fused-prefill advice still says “increase108of110cores.” This is an aggregate-count limitation:88MM+20CCL, leaving two cores that cannot extend the current origin-anchored rectangular MM grid while retaining CCL at row8.11x9 overlaps the CCL allocation;12x8 exceeds the11-wide worker grid. The measured8/10/11-wide native family closes the applicable geometry search. This is not a proof that every possible CCL relocation or kernel redesign is impossible.
