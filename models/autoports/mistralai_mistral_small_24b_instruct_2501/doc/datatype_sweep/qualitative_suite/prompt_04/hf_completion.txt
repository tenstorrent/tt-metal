The three laws of thermodynamics are fundamental principles that describe how energy moves and changes form. Here they are:

1. **Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics**: If two systems are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other. This law helps define the concept of temperature.

2. **First Law of Thermodynamics**: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, but it can be transformed from one form to another or transferred from one object to another. This is often expressed as the conservation of energy.
