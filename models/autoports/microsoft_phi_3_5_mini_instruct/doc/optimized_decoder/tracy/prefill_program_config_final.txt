Conclusion
----------
The final decoder retains automatic large configs for QKV/output/gate-up and
uses the proven explicit 32-core (B1) or 64-core (B32) block8 down config.
The larger-block experiment and exact QKV/gate-up B32 L1 blockers are preserved
in `../prefill_explicit_config_runner.txt`. The profiler shows no host fallback
or Torch conversion.
